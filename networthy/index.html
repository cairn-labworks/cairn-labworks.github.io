Networthy

Private net-worth tracker

Networthy tracks your net worth: everything you own minus everything you owe, organised into portfolios and shown in one base currency.

  • Stocks, funds, crypto, gold, cash, deposits, property, vehicles, bonds and more.
  • Loans, mortgages, credit cards, pending payments and taxes.
  • Latest prices by ticker symbol, and automatic currency conversion.
  • Multiple portfolios, charts by category, and light, dark and dynamic-colour themes.
  • Password-protected encrypted backups that you export and import yourself.

Your data is encrypted on your device and never leaves it. Networthy has no account, no ads and no analytics. Read the privacy policy, or see the source code.
