Networthy Privacy Policy

Effective 5 October 2026

Networthy (package com.cairnlabworks.mywealth) is a net-worth tracker for Android and iOS made by Cairn Labworks ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

In short: Networthy keeps your financial data on your device, encrypted. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting. It goes online only to look up public market prices and exchange rates, and sends nothing but a ticker symbol or a currency code. We never receive your data, and we never sell it or use it for ad targeting.

Information stored on your device

The assets, liabilities, portfolios and settings you enter stay on your device:

  • They are kept in a database encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). Its key is created on your device and protected by the Android Keystore or the iOS Keychain.
  • On Android, the app's data is excluded from Android backups and device-to-device transfers, so it doesn't leave your device that way.
  • They stay until you delete them in the app or uninstall Networthy, which deletes them.

Internet use

Networthy works offline. It connects to the internet only for these lookups:

  • Market prices from Yahoo Finance (query1.finance.yahoo.com): the app sends the ticker symbol of an asset, for example AAPL, only when you fetch or refresh its price.
  • Exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com): the app sends your base currency code, for example USD, automatically on first launch and then at most once every 12 hours.

Your portfolio names, holdings, quantities and values are never sent. As with any internet request, these services can see your IP address and the app's name and platform, for example Networthy/1.0 (Android). Their own privacy policies apply to that information. We don't receive it.

Encrypted backups

If you export a backup, Networthy encrypts it with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM, with the key derived using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) and saves it wherever you pick. We never receive your backups or your password, and we can't recover a forgotten password.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the price and exchange-rate lookups above.
  • Biometrics (optional): for the app lock. Your device checks your fingerprint, face or PIN; Networthy only learns whether the check succeeded and never receives biometric data.

Sharing

  • We don't share, sell or rent information to anyone, because we don't receive any.
  • If you save or share an exported backup with another app or service, that app's privacy policy applies to what you share.
  • Google may collect information when you download Networthy or use Google Play, under Google's privacy policy.

Children

Networthy is a personal finance tool for adults and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Your data is encrypted on your device, and you can add an app lock and hide balances on screen. Keep your device locked and your backup password safe.

Your choices

  • You can delete any item or portfolio in the app at any time, or uninstall Networthy to remove everything.
  • You can turn the app lock on or off in Networthy's settings, and allow or deny permissions in your device settings.
  • We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Networthy handles information differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
