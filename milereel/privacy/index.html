MileReel Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

MileReel (package com.cairnlabworks.milereel) is a dashcam app for Android made by Cairn Labworks ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and what happens to it.

In short: MileReel does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting, and it doesn't have permission to use the internet, so nothing it records can leave your device through the app. We never receive your recordings, your location or anything else about you.

What the app uses, and why

MileReel only uses these on your device, to make its features work:

  • Camera: to record video. The camera is used only while recording is on, which you start yourself, or which the optional "Start recording when charging" setting starts when your phone begins charging. While recording, a notification and a floating REC bubble are always shown.
  • Microphone: to record sound with your videos. You can turn this off in Settings ("Record audio").
  • Location (optional): to stamp the date, time, GPS position and speed onto your videos, to save the last known position in each video file's metadata, to show your speed in Live view, and to help tell whether the car is moving (for auto-pause). Location is used only while recording. If you don't allow location access, MileReel still records, without position and speed.
  • Motion sensors: to notice when the car has been parked, for auto-pause.
  • Battery level, charging state and temperature: for charger auto-start and auto-stop, and for the overheating, low-battery and charge-limit protection.
  • Storage: to save recordings as video files in the Movies/MileReel folder on your phone, or on an SD card if you choose. Android 10 and older ask for storage permission for this.
  • Display over other apps: for the floating REC bubble, and to start recording when the charger is connected.
  • Notifications: to show that recording is running and to give safety alerts.
  • Do Not Disturb access (optional): to silence the system chime while recording.
  • Camera placement check (experimental, off unless you turn it on): looks at the video frames on your device to warn you if the camera is aimed too high. Frames are not saved or sent anywhere.

Where your information is stored, and for how long

  • Recordings are video files on your device. They stay until you delete them, or until loop recording replaces the oldest unstarred clips to stay within the storage limit you set. Starred clips are kept until you delete them.
  • Recordings are saved in your shared Movies folder, so your gallery and file manager, and other apps you have allowed to access videos, can see them. They also stay on your device if you uninstall MileReel. The exception is Android 10 and older when saving to an SD card: there Android only allows the app's own folder on the card, and deletes it when the app is uninstalled.
  • Videos include the date, time and, if location is allowed, the position and speed of the car, both on the picture and in the file. Anyone you share a video with can see these.
  • Settings and your list of starred clips are kept in the app's private storage and are deleted when you uninstall MileReel. If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include them in your device backup, under Google's privacy policy. Recordings are never included in the app's backups.
  • Diagnostics: the app keeps a short list of recent events (such as "recording started") in memory and in your device's system log, to help with troubleshooting. It is not sent anywhere.

Sharing

  • We don't share information with anyone, because we don't receive any.
  • If you open or share a recording with another app, such as a video player or a messaging app, that app's privacy policy applies to what you share.
  • If "Start Maps in background" is on (the default), MileReel opens Google Maps in driving mode when recording starts. MileReel doesn't pass any of your information to Maps; Google's privacy policy applies to your use of Maps.
  • Google may collect information when you download MileReel or use Google Play, under Google's privacy policy.

Recording other people

Your recordings may capture other people, vehicles, number plates and conversations. You are responsible for using MileReel and your recordings lawfully. Rules on recording video and sound, and on using a phone while driving, differ between countries.

Children

MileReel is meant for drivers and is not directed at children. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Security

Because your recordings and settings stay on your device, they are protected by your device's own security, such as its screen lock and encryption. Keep your device locked if your recordings are private.

Your choices

  • You can allow or deny each permission, and change your mind at any time in Android Settings > Apps > MileReel > Permissions. MileReel can't record without camera access.
  • You can turn off sound recording in MileReel's settings.
  • You can delete recordings at any time in MileReel (Recordings, or Settings > Delete all recordings), or with your gallery or file manager.
  • We hold no information about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on your behalf. Everything is under your control on your device.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of MileReel handles information differently, we will update this page and its effective date before that version is released.
