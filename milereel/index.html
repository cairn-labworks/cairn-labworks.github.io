MileReel

Phone dashcam for Android

MileReel turns your Android phone into a dashcam. Mount the phone, tap Record and drive. MileReel keeps recording in the background while you use navigation, music or any other app, and it loops within the storage space you choose.

  • Loop recording in clips of 1, 3, 5 or 10 minutes; star the clips you want to keep.
  • Starts recording when the car powers on and stops when it powers off.
  • Date, time, GPS position and speed stamped onto the video.
  • Recordings are saved in Movies/MileReel on your phone or SD card, and stay there even if you uninstall the app.
  • Overheating, low-battery and charge-limit protection.

Everything stays on your phone. MileReel has no account, no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't use the internet. Read the privacy policy.
